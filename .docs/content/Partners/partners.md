# Partner List

The Partner List is where you manage all of your customers and vendors. A partner can be a customer, a vendor, or both.

{{< screenshot src="../../screenshots/partners/partner-list.png" alt="Partner List Screenshot" />}}

## The Partner Table

The main table on this page displays all your partners with the following columns:

- **Legal Name**: The official, legal name of the partner.
- **Trade Name**: The name the partner does business as, if different from their legal name.
- **Type**: Indicates whether the partner is a "Customer," "Vendor," or "Vendor & Customer."
- **Actions**: A set of controls for managing the partner.

## Managing Partners

You can manage your partners directly from this screen.

### Adding a New Partner

To create a new partner, click the **Add Partner** button located at the top of the page.

- For detailed instructions, see the [Add New Partner](../add-partner/) page.

### Editing a Partner

To modify a partner's details, addresses, or contacts, click the **Edit** button in the "Actions" column for that
partner row. This will open the Partner Drawer.

- For detailed instructions, see the [Edit Partner](../edit-partner/) page.

### Deleting a Partner

To remove a partner, click the **Delete** button in the "Actions" column. Please note that you can only delete partners
that have no transactions associated with them. If a partner has a history, the delete button will be disabled.

- For more information, see the [Delete Partner](../delete-partner/) page.
