# Editing a Partner

To edit an existing partner, click the **Edit** button in the "Actions" column of the partner you wish to change on the
main [Partner List](../partners/) page. This will open the "Partner Drawer," a side panel where you can manage all
aspects of the partner.

{% screenshot() %}
![Edit Partner Screenshot](../../screenshots/partners/edit-partner.png)
{% end %}

The Partner Drawer has three tabs:

- **General**: For the partner's main details.
- **Addresses**: For managing the partner's physical and mailing addresses.
- **Contacts**: For managing the people you interact with at the partner's organization.

## General Tab

The "General" tab allows you to modify the same information you provided when you created the partner:

- **Legal Name**
- **Trade Name**
- **Tax Identifier**
- **Is Vendor** / **Is Customer**
- **Default AP Account**
- **Default AR Account**

Click the **Save** button at the bottom of the drawer to save any changes you make.
