# Profit & Loss Statement

The Profit & Loss (P&L) statement, also known as the Income Statement, summarizes your company's revenues and expenses over a specific period of time to show its profitability.

{% screenshot() %}
![Profit & Loss Screenshot](../../screenshots/reports/profit-loss.png)
{% end %}

## Generating the Report

To generate the report, select a start and end date in the **Report Options** header. The report will calculate the total revenue and expenses within this period.

## Report Structure

The P&L statement is divided into two main sections:

- **Revenue**: This section lists all your revenue accounts and their balances for the selected period.
- **Expenses**: This section lists all your expense accounts and their balances.

Like the Trial Balance, the P&L is a hierarchical report. You can click the toggle button next to any parent account to expand or collapse it. You can also click on any non-group account name to navigate directly to that account's ledger.

### Net Income

The report concludes with the **Net Income**, which is the final calculation of your company's profit or loss for the period (Total Revenue - Total Expenses).
